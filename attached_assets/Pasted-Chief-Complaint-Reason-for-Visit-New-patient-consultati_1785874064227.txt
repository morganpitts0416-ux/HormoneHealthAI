We also reviewed the benefits of initiating a transdermal estrogen patch to manage her low energy, frequent hot flashes, and joint aches. The estrogen patch provides stable hormone levels and supports cardiac and cognitive function. Additionally, we recommended starting testosterone injections to address her low energy and libido.

We also discussed increasing her vitamin D intake to 10,000 IU daily with K2 to enhance cognitive function, energy, and hormonal balance. Other supplements such as Estrovera for vasomotor symptoms, CoQ10 for muscle recovery and cardiac protection, and omega-3 fish oils for general health were explored, but no definitive decisions were made regarding these.
