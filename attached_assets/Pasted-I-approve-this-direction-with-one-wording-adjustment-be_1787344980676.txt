Medication actions, doses/routes/frequencies, safety-critical information, patient decisions affecting treatment, orders, monitoring, and follow-up must still appear wherever clinically required.

The goal is not fewer clinical facts. The goal is that each section performs its own job:

HPI → detailed clinical story and encounter context
Overall Clinical Impression → focused synthesis of the clinical picture and principal outcome
Clinical Rationale → decision-relevant evidence and reasoning for that specific problem
Plan → clinical actions, medications, orders, monitoring, follow-up
Care Plan → patient-actionable instructions and safety information

A fact may appropriately appear in more than one section, but the full narrative should not be repeated merely to satisfy a coverage requirement.
