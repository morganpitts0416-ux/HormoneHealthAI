Question/next refinement for Spruce June behavior:

With the current workflow gates, I want to confirm whether Spruce June only responds when a message matches a configured workflow.

Long-term, I do want Spruce June to acknowledge most inbound patient messages, even when they do not clearly fall into a specific workflow.

However, I do NOT want her to become an unrestricted medical advice chatbot.

Desired behavior:

1. Specific workflow match
   If the message clearly matches a workflow, use that workflow’s acknowledgment/intake behavior.

Examples:

* refill request → collect medication/pharmacy/missing refill details
* scheduling request → collect preferred days/times
* lab question → acknowledge and route to clinical team
* urgent concern → emergency language + escalation
* missed call → missed-call follow-up

2. No specific workflow match / general message
   If the message is inbound from a patient but does not confidently match a workflow, Spruce June should still be able to send a general acknowledgment if enabled.

Example:
“Hey {firstName}, I’ll get this message over to the care team so they can help. A team member will follow up as soon as possible.”

This should create a general staff-facing memo/task, not disappear silently.

3. Intuitive response, not generic canned response
   Even for general messages, June should read the message and respond in a way that makes sense.

Examples:

Patient:
“Hey, I have a quick question.”
June:
“Hey Sarah — I’ll get this over to the care team so they can help. A team member will follow up as soon as possible.”

Patient:
“I think I have a UTI. Can someone help?”
June:
“Hey Sarah — I’ll get this to the nurse for review. To help them follow up, when did your symptoms start, and are you having fever, chills, back pain, or blood in your urine?”

Patient:
“I’m running late.”
June:
“Thanks for letting us know — I’ll pass this along to the team.”

Patient:
“Can someone call me?”
June:
“Of course — I’ll let the team know you’d like a call back. Is there a good number or time to reach you?”

4. Safety boundaries
   General acknowledgment mode must still follow all safety gates:

* inbound patient/external messages only
* clinic Spruce integration enabled
* Spruce June acknowledgments enabled
* conversation not in staff_takeover / ai_muted
* no staff reply after the patient message
* max June turns respected
* no diagnosis
* no prescribing
* no broad medical advice
* emergency escalation language when appropriate

5. Settings
   Please add/plan a clinic-level or workflow-level setting for:
   General Message Acknowledgment

Default OFF.

This allows clinics to decide whether Spruce June acknowledges unclassified/general messages.

6. Dashboard/memo routing
   General messages should create a staff-facing memo/task routed to a general Patient Messages / Clinical Inbox queue, not the medication refill queue.

Memo should include:

* patient
* original message
* June acknowledgment sent
* what June asked, if anything
* suggested staff follow-up
* urgency/priority
* link to conversation
* link to chart if matched

7. Important
   If confidence is low or the message could be clinical/urgent, June should acknowledge and escalate rather than trying to answer.

Keep this isolated to Spruce June / Spruce Inbox workflows only.
Do not modify existing ClinIQ June, Ask June, SOAP, transcription, lab evaluation, evidence overlay, auth, billing, sessions, or unrelated core systems.
