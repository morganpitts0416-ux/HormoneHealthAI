IMPORTANT PLACEMENT

Do NOT create a separate mitochondrial disease module.

Add this assessment inside the same Metabolic Health Assessment section that currently contains:

* Insulin Resistance Score
* Metabolic Phenotype Classification
* Metabolic Recommendations

The Cellular Energy / Mito Score should be displayed directly below the insulin resistance assessment.

CLINICAL INTENT

This assessment is designed to identify patterns associated with impaired cellular energy production, poor recovery, exercise intolerance, fatigue, brain fog, and metabolic dysfunction.

This assessment DOES NOT diagnose primary mitochondrial disease.

The purpose is to identify modifiable contributors to impaired cellular energy production using conventional laboratory markers already collected in ClinIQ.

SCORING METHODOLOGY

Only score available markers.

Do not require all markers to be present.

Display:

* Score achieved
* Maximum possible score based on available labs
* Percentage score
* Missing markers

CALCULATE:

Mito Score = Patient Points ÷ Maximum Available Points × 100

─────────────────────────────
DOMAIN 1: INFLAMMATION
─────────────────────────────

hs-CRP

* <1.0 = 0 points
* 1.0–2.9 = 1 point
* ≥3.0 = 2 points

─────────────────────────────
DOMAIN 2: IRON AVAILABILITY
─────────────────────────────

Ferritin

* 75–150 = 0 points
* 50–74 = 1 point
* <50 = 2 points

Special Rule:

If ferritin >150 and hs-CRP is elevated:

* assign 1 point

Reason:
Ferritin may represent inflammation rather than optimal iron availability.

─────────────────────────────
DOMAIN 3: B12
─────────────────────────────

Vitamin B12

* > 600 = 0 points
* 450–600 = 1 point
* <450 = 2 points

─────────────────────────────
DOMAIN 4: FOLATE
─────────────────────────────

Folate

* > 10 = 0 points
* 6–10 = 1 point
* <6 = 2 points

─────────────────────────────
DOMAIN 5: VITAMIN D
─────────────────────────────

Vitamin D

* > 50 = 0 points
* 30–50 = 1 point
* <30 = 2 points

─────────────────────────────
DOMAIN 6: MAGNESIUM
─────────────────────────────

If RBC magnesium available:

* optimal = 0 points
* low-normal = 1 point
* low = 2 points

If serum magnesium only:

* ≥2.0 = 0 points
* 1.8–1.9 = 1 point
* <1.8 = 2 points

─────────────────────────────
DOMAIN 7: THYROID SUPPORT
─────────────────────────────

Use TSH, Free T4, and Free T3 when available.

* TSH 0.5–2.5 with normal FT4/FT3 = 0 points
* TSH 2.6–4.0 OR low-normal FT3 = 1 point
* TSH >4.0 OR low FT4 OR low FT3 = 2 points

─────────────────────────────
DOMAIN 8: INSULIN RESISTANCE BURDEN
─────────────────────────────

Use the existing ClinIQ Insulin Resistance Score.

* Low IR = 0 points
* Early/Emerging IR = 1 point
* Moderate or High IR = 2 points

Reason:
Insulin resistance is a major driver of impaired mitochondrial function.

─────────────────────────────
DOMAIN 9: LIPID / APOB BURDEN
─────────────────────────────

* ApoB <80 AND TG <2 = 0 points
* ApoB 80–89 OR TG 2–2.9 = 1 point
* ApoB ≥90 OR TG ≥3 = 2 points

─────────────────────────────
DOMAIN 10: CBC / OXYGEN DELIVERY
─────────────────────────────

Evaluate:

* Hemoglobin
* Hematocrit
* MCV
* RDW

Scoring:

Normal values = 0 points

Borderline:

* mild anemia
* elevated RDW
* mild macrocytosis
* mild microcytosis

= 1 point

Significant abnormalities:

* anemia
* marked macrocytosis
* marked microcytosis

= 2 points

DOMAIN 11: HORMONAL SUPPORT (FEMALES)
─────────────────────────────

Clinical Rationale

Hormonal status influences:

* mitochondrial biogenesis
* muscle maintenance
* recovery
* cognition
* metabolic flexibility
* cellular energy production

TESTOSTERONE HIERARCHY

Use:

1. Free Testosterone (preferred)
2. Calculated Free Testosterone
3. Bioavailable Testosterone
4. Total Testosterone interpreted with SHBG

Never interpret Total Testosterone alone.

─────────────────────────────
ESTRADIOL
─────────────────────────────

Postmenopausal Women

* Estradiol >50 = 0 points
* Estradiol 20–50 = 1 point
* Estradiol <20 = 2 points

Perimenopausal Women

Do not score based on a single estradiol level.

Instead evaluate:

* vasomotor symptoms
* sleep disruption
* anxiety
* cycle irregularity
* fatigue
* poor recovery

Mild symptoms = 1 point

Moderate/Severe symptoms = 2 points

─────────────────────────────
FREE TESTOSTERONE
─────────────────────────────

Use clinic-defined optimal range.

* Optimal Free Testosterone = 0 points
* Borderline Low Free Testosterone = 1 point
* Clearly Low Free Testosterone = 2 points

Only assign testosterone-related points if symptoms are present:

* fatigue
* poor recovery
* reduced strength
* muscle loss
* exercise intolerance
* low motivation

─────────────────────────────
SHBG MODIFIER
─────────────────────────────

DO NOT allow SHBG to independently determine the Mito Score.

Use SHBG as a phenotype confidence modifier only.

Female SHBG:

* 40–80 = neutral
* <40 = increases metabolic confidence
* > 100 = increases hormonal confidence

Interpretation:

Low SHBG may reflect:

* insulin resistance
* hyperinsulinemia
* fatty liver
* visceral adiposity
* androgen excess

High SHBG may reflect:

* oral estrogen use
* hyperthyroidism
* reduced androgen availability

Adjustment Rules:

If:

* Free Testosterone low
  AND
* SHBG >100

Add +1 confidence point to Thyroid/Hormonal Pattern.

If:

* Free Testosterone low
  AND
* SHBG <40
  AND
* insulin resistance phenotype present

Do NOT increase Hormonal Pattern.

Increase Metabolic Pattern confidence instead.

If:

* Free Testosterone normal
  AND
* SHBG abnormal

Assign no testosterone deficiency points.

─────────────────────────────
PATTERN CONFIDENCE SCORING
─────────────────────────────

Nutrient Depletion Pattern

Increase confidence for:

* Ferritin <75
* B12 <600
* Folate <10
* Vitamin D <50
* Magnesium suboptimal

Inflammatory Pattern

Increase confidence for:

* hs-CRP ≥2
* Ferritin >150 with elevated hs-CRP
* known inflammatory disease

Metabolic Pattern

Increase confidence for:

* Moderate/High IR
* ApoB ≥90
* TG ≥3
* Elevated ALT
* Central obesity
* Low SHBG

Thyroid/Hormonal Pattern

Increase confidence for:

* TSH >2.5
* Low FT3
* Low FT4
* Low Estradiol
* Symptomatic perimenopause
* Low Free Testosterone with elevated SHBG

Oxygen Delivery Pattern

Increase confidence for:

* anemia
* elevated RDW
* macrocytosis
* microcytosis
* low ferritin with CBC changes

─────────────────────────────
TIE BREAKER HIERARCHY
─────────────────────────────

If tie exists:

1. Oxygen Delivery wins if anemia present
2. Metabolic wins if moderate/high IR present
3. Inflammatory wins if hs-CRP ≥3
4. Nutrient Depletion wins if 3+ nutrient abnormalities present
5. Thyroid/Hormonal wins if thyroid or hormone abnormalities dominate

─────────────────────────────
SAFETY RULES
─────────────────────────────

Suppress recommendations if:

* pregnancy
* breastfeeding
* significant kidney disease
* significant liver disease
* anticoagulant therapy
* hypoglycemia risk
* diabetes medication interaction risk
* upcoming surgery
* allergy/intolerance
* hemochromatosis/iron overload
* active cancer treatment unless approved
