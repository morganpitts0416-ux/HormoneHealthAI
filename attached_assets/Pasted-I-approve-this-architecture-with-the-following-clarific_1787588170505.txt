I approve this architecture with the following clarifications before implementation:

1. Canonical field semantics

Implement lab_results.patient_communication_summary as the single authoritative current patient-facing Health Assessment for each lab evaluation. Do not require a separate formal approval state.

On initial evaluation, the AI-generated Patient Communication Summary may populate this field. Once a clinician edits and saves it, that edited value becomes the authoritative current content.

All patient-facing/rendering consumers must use this same canonical value:

provider-side Patient Communication Summary editor
Patient Portal → Health Assessment
provider lab-report PDF Patient-Friendly Summary, if that section is retained
patient Wellness PDF → Understanding Your Results

The PDF must not independently regenerate “Understanding Your Results.”

2. Complete the ClinIQ Brain before generating communication

Reorder generation as proposed so the communication writer receives the completed structured evaluation, including all applicable interpretations, screening results, phenotypes/patterns, cardiovascular reasoning, IR reasoning, hormone reasoning, mito findings, supplements with doses/rationales, recommendations, red flags, therapy context, trends when relevant, and recheck timing.

3. Brain authority with additive evidence-based reasoning

For findings covered by structured ClinIQ Brain logic, the Brain's interpretation and management recommendation are authoritative clinical context. The communication writer must not independently substitute a conflicting recommendation, dose, target, classification, or treatment.

However, the ClinIQ Brain is authoritative where it has adjudicated a finding; it is not assumed to be exhaustive of medicine.

For clinically relevant findings not covered by an existing ClinIQ Brain rule, the writer may provide evidence-based, nuanced interpretation and recommendations using the available clinical context.

It must not manufacture diagnoses, certainty, treatment decisions, or causal relationships unsupported by the data.

4. Clinical selection, not Brain dumping

The writer should have access to the complete patient-visible Brain output but should not mechanically narrate every marker, algorithm, or normal result.

Write a useful patient communication prioritizing:

important abnormal or actionable findings;
clinically meaningful patterns and screening results;
what those findings mean for this patient;
treatment/supplement/lifestyle recommendations generated by the evaluation;
medication or therapy changes when applicable;
monitoring/recheck plan;
important reassuring findings when clinically useful.

The goal is to explain what matters, what it means, what we're doing, and what happens next.

5. Provider-to-patient voice

Write as the treating provider speaking directly to the patient.

Appropriate language includes:

“Your labs show…”
“I recommend…”
“I'd like you to…”
“We're going to…”
“We'll recheck…”
“Our next step is…”

Never use:

“ask your provider”
“discuss with your provider”
“consult your clinician”
“your healthcare provider may…”
“your care team…”

6. Provider edits always win

Never allow lab re-evaluation, PDF generation, portal publication, wellness-plan generation, or background AI processes to silently overwrite a clinician-edited canonical communication.

If underlying clinical information changes after a clinician edit, preserve the existing communication and require an explicit clinician action to regenerate/replace it. We can add “Suggest additions” or diff functionality later; that does not need to be part of this first implementation.

7. Visibility

The communication writer must respect provider-hidden/provider-only findings and recommendations. Hidden content must not leak back into the patient communication or PDF through a separate AI generation path.

8. Keep encounter Visit Summary separate

Do not alter clinical_encounters.patient_summary or the SOAP → Visit Summary workflow. This project applies only to the lab Health Assessment system.

Before coding, give me the minimal implementation plan, including schema migration/backfill strategy, functions/routes that will change, generation-order changes, and exactly which PDF/portal consumers will be redirected to the canonical field. Do not implement until I approve the plan.
