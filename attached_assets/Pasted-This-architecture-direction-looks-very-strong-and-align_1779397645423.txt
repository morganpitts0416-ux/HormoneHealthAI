This architecture direction looks very strong and aligned with my vision for Spruce June.

A few important strategic clarifications before moving into Phase 2:

1. Spruce June should fundamentally be a workflow orchestration system, not a freeform chatbot.
   The AI should operate inside workflow guardrails and state machines, not as an unrestricted conversational medical assistant.

2. Long-term, workflows should become composable and clinic-configurable.
   Not just hardcoded workflows like refill/scheduling, but eventually:

* GLP-1 follow-up
* BHRT follow-up
* post-procedure aftercare
* membership/billing workflows
* redraw/lab follow-up
* etc.

I want clinics to eventually configure:

* triggers
* required information
* escalation rules
* allowed AI actions
* task generation
* workflow toggles

without needing engineering changes.

3. Please architect conversation state with awareness that a single conversation may eventually contain multiple simultaneous workflow contexts.
   Not asking to build multi-workflow orchestration yet — just avoid assumptions that one thread always equals one workflow forever.

4. Future AI retrieval should be structured and workflow-scoped.
   I do NOT want Spruce June freely accessing entire patient charts.
   Workflow-specific retrieval later should expose only the minimum necessary contextual data for that workflow.

5. The Inbox itself is becoming a major platform surface.
   Please think of this as evolving into a unified clinical communication workspace:

* Spruce
* patient messaging
* workflow tasks
* AI summaries
* refill coordination
* scheduling coordination
* escalations
* staff takeover states

All while preserving strict human control and auditability.

Importantly:
Please continue keeping Spruce June isolated from the existing ClinIQ June assistant and do not modify:

* Ask June
* SOAP generation
* transcription
* lab evaluation
* evidence overlay
* patient summaries
* auth
* billing
* scheduling
* core ClinIQ systems

I agree with the proposed phased rollout:

* staff replies from ClinIQ first
* AI drafts second
* limited workflow auto-replies later

The human takeover/sticky mute architecture is exactly the right direction.
