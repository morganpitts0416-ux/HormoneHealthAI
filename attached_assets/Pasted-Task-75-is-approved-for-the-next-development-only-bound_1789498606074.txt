Task #75 is approved for the next development-only boundary.

Implement synthetic-only enforcement for a narrowly reviewed allowlist of ordinary clinic-scoped routes.

Required architecture
Preserve finalAllow = legacyAllow && proposedClinicAccessAllow.
The new policy may restrict a legacy allow but may never convert a legacy denial into an allow.
Middleware must execute after existing authentication.
Existing route-specific role, ownership, resource, and validation checks remain in place.
Do not replace current authorization with the resolver.
Indeterminate results must not authorize anything denied by legacy behavior.
Route allowlist

Before implementation, enumerate the exact routes included.

Exclude:

Patient, chart, document, download, lab, encounter, and other resource-ID routes
Jobs, workflows, queued AI work, and integrations
Billing and cancellation
Owner, admin, staff, provider, invitation, and team-management routes
Patient portal and patient-intake routes
Platform admin and operations portals
Public, token-bound, bootstrap, webhook, and system-secret routes
Login, logout, /api/auth/me, membership listing, and explicit clinic reselection

Do not broadly attach enforcement to all 251 classified routes. Start with a small explicit allowlist whose handlers require clinic context but do not accept patient/resource identifiers.

Feature flags

Use the ordinary-clinic-access family flags already defined:

Shadow flag defaults absent/off.
Enforcement flag defaults absent/off.
Do not create an enabled flag row.
Missing or failed flag resolution must preserve current behavior.

Actual Express enforcement may operate only for explicitly synthetic actors during validation. No real development user may be denied.

Required authenticated HTTP tests

Test through the real Express middleware and session stack—not only the pure policy function:

Active clinician succeeds.
Active staff succeeds only where the route permits staff.
Revoked clinician’s stale cookie is denied.
Revoked staff’s stale cookie is denied.
Missing membership is denied.
Stale activeClinicId is denied.
Stale defaultClinicId does not authorize.
Clinic A revocation does not prevent explicit selection and use of active Clinic B.
The application never silently switches clinics.
Staff/clinic mismatch is denied.
Browser clinic headers, query parameters, bodies, and unsigned cookies cannot override server context.
A legacy denial remains denied even when the resolver returns allow.
Flag disabled, absent, expired, or unreadable reproduces current behavior exactly.

Use uniform sanitized actor-level denial responses. Do not reveal whether another clinic or membership exists.

Compatibility protections

With flags off:

Existing clinician and staff login behavior remains unchanged.
Existing sessions remain unchanged.
Patient portal behavior remains unchanged.
Billing and the emergency cancellation patch remain untouched.
Account 2 and all identity/relationship records remain unchanged.
No ownership, membership, provider, staff, session, patient, password, or clinic record changes.

All synthetic users, memberships, staff records, clinics, providers, and sessions must be removed or transactionally rolled back.

Stop conditions

Stop if the allowlisted middleware requires changes to session format, login, password handling, clinic selection, patient authorization, or current real-user behavior.

Do not add team-management UI or mutation endpoints.
Do not deactivate or reactivate real users.
Do not resolve existing ownership or membership findings automatically.
Do not enable enforcement for real accounts.
Do not merge the cancellation branch.
Do not push, publish, or deploy.

Report the exact allowlisted routes, middleware placement, flag behavior, authenticated HTTP results, database before/after counts, compatibility results, and the next proposed route family.
