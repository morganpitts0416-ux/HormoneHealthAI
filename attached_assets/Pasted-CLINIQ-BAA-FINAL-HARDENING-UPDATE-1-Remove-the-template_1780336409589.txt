CLINIQ BAA FINAL HARDENING UPDATE

1. Remove the "template" legal review banner and replace with:

"This Business Associate Agreement constitutes a legally binding agreement governing the handling of Protected Health Information between ClinIQ and the Covered Entity."

2. Expand Section 3.1 (Permitted Uses) to include:

* clinical documentation
* encounter transcription
* encounter summarization
* workflow automation
* patient communications
* patient portal functionality
* scheduling support
* telehealth support where enabled
* AI-assisted documentation and organizational functions

3. Add new section:

"Subprocessors"

ClinIQ may engage subcontractors and service providers to support hosting, infrastructure, artificial intelligence processing, communications delivery, security services, and platform operations.

ClinIQ shall maintain written agreements requiring such subcontractors to comply with applicable HIPAA obligations when PHI is involved.

4. Add new section:

"De-Identified Information"

ClinIQ may create and use de-identified information in accordance with HIPAA.

Such information may be used for analytics, quality improvement, product development, operational monitoring, security, and platform improvement.

ClinIQ shall not attempt to re-identify de-identified information except as permitted by law.

5. Add new section:

"Security Incidents"

ClinIQ shall investigate security incidents involving PHI and take commercially reasonable measures to mitigate known harmful effects.

6. Add new section:

"Data Return and Export"

Covered Entity may request export of available data during active subscription and within applicable post-termination periods.

7. Add new section:

"Non-BAA Vendors"

ClinIQ does not intentionally disclose PHI to service providers that are not operating under appropriate contractual protections when PHI processing is required.

8. Expand No Third-Party Beneficiary language:

Patients, patient representatives, end users, and other third parties are not parties to this BAA and acquire no rights under it.

9. Add:

"Survival"

Confidentiality, security, breach notification, retained PHI obligations, and HIPAA-related protections survive termination.

10. Add:

"Cooperation"

ClinIQ shall reasonably cooperate with Covered Entity investigations involving HIPAA compliance, security incidents, and breach response.

TERMS OF SERVICE FOLLOW-UP CHANGES

1. Add:

"To the extent any conflict exists between these Terms and an executed Business Associate Agreement, the Business Associate Agreement shall control with respect to Protected Health Information."

2. Replace any statement that Customer is solely responsible for PHI with:

"Customer remains responsible for lawful collection, authorization, and permitted disclosure of PHI, while ClinIQ remains responsible for obligations expressly assumed under the applicable Business Associate Agreement."
