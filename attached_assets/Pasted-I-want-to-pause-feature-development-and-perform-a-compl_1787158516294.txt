## PART 5 — AUDIT THE EVIDENCE LAYER

The intended Evidence Layer behavior is:

After a SOAP note is generated, the provider should see evidence attached to diagnoses, recommendations, medications, interventions, and counseling.

There should be brief evidence "pills" adjacent to relevant SOAP content, for example:

"This intervention is supported by [guideline/source]. [Medication/intervention] is recommended for..."

The provider should also be able to open the full Evidence tab/page and see ALL evidence used to support that SOAP note.

The hierarchy should be:

### When ClinIQ Brain HAS a rule/recommendation

The Evidence Layer should SUPPORT the ClinIQ Brain with the legitimate evidence underlying that rule.

It should NOT independently challenge or contradict the ClinIQ Brain.

### When ClinIQ Brain DOES NOT cover a diagnosis/condition/intervention

The Evidence Layer should be ADDITIVE:

* retrieve current reputable evidence
* provide supporting guideline/research information
* identify relevant additional evidence-based considerations
* cite the source

* How evidence pills are rendered
* How the Evidence page is populated
* What is stored in the database
* What is generated dynamically
* Whether evidence generation is currently being called during SOAP generation
* Whether evidence results are currently being persisted
