This direction sounds much closer to what I’m wanting. I do NOT want minimalist or under-documented notes though, so we need to be careful not to overcorrect.

The goal is still strong medicolegal completeness, documentation of patient education, shared decision-making, informed consent, monitoring plans, and treatment rationale — especially for hormones, GLP-1s, thyroid management, compounded medications, and metabolic optimization.

What I want removed is the generic AI filler and over-narration.

Examples of what I DO want reduced:

repetitive “patient verbalized understanding” phrasing
generic counseling wrappers
over-explaining obvious lab findings
unnecessary anecdotal storytelling
bloated narrative language

But I still want clinically meaningful counseling documented.

For example:
Instead of:
“Discussed importance of vitamin D in reducing inflammation and improving energy levels.”

I want:
“Reviewed goal vitamin D range and discussed potential contribution of suboptimal levels to fatigue and metabolic health. Patient agreeable to supplementation and repeat monitoring.”

That keeps the medicolegal protection while sounding more provider-authentic and clinically intelligent.

A few important additions/modifications:

Avoid unnecessary personal anecdotes unless they meaningfully clarify symptom severity, functional impairment, or diagnostic reasoning.
Do not reduce clinical completeness or medicolegal defensibility in pursuit of conciseness.
Document clinically meaningful counseling, informed consent discussions, treatment rationale, monitoring expectations, and shared decision-making.
Avoid repetitive generic filler phrasing that lacks specific clinical relevance.
The Assessment & Plan should sound like the provider independently interpreted the data and formed a clinical impression — not merely restated the transcript.

I still want the notes to feel high-touch and thoughtful — just more like an experienced concierge hormone/metabolic medicine provider documenting efficiently and confidently, rather than an AI medical explainer or transcriptionist.
