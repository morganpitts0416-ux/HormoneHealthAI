Before proceeding with Phase 2, I want to clarify one important requirement:

We need internal staff messaging / internal notes inside the patient communication timeline.

Use case:
If I am reviewing a patient message and need to tell my nurse something like:
“@Sarah can you call her and confirm her progesterone dose before we refill?”
I want to be able to tag a staff member so they are notified.

This internal message/note should:

* appear chronologically inside the patient communication timeline for staff
* be clearly labeled “Internal”
* never be visible to the patient in the portal
* support @mentions/tagging of staff members
* notify the tagged staff member
* remain tied to the patient and/or conversation context
* be auditable

This means we need a clear distinction between:

1. Patient-visible communications
   Examples:

* portal messages
* Spruce SMS messages
* staff replies to patient
* Spruce June patient-facing replies

2. Internal-only communication/events
   Examples:

* internal staff notes
* @mentions
* nurse handoff notes
* workflow routing notes
* June memos
* escalation notes
* assignment notes

Please add/future-proof the architecture with fields such as:

* visibility: patient_visible | internal_only
  or
* isPatientVisible boolean

Important:
Internal-only messages must NEVER show in the patient portal, but they should show in the staff-facing patient chart timeline and Inbox context.

Also, when adding internal notes:

* support @staff mentions
* create a notification/task for the tagged user
* preserve audit trail with createdByUserId, createdAt, patientId, conversation/context if available

This should fit into the unified communication timeline concept.

I still agree with the overall Phase 2 direction:

* portal_messages becomes the longitudinal patient-visible communication record
* Spruce remains a delivery channel
* Inbox remains the operational workspace
* Patient chart remains the patient-specific workspace

But we must not accidentally expose internal notes, workflow memos, or staff tags to patients.

Please account for this before building Phase 2.
