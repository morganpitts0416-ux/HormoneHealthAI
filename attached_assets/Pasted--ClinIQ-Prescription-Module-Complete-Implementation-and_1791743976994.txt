## 2. Finish the prescriber verification workflow

The backend already supports prescriber credential verification, but an administrator-facing verification interface is missing.

Build a secure interface under Settings → Prescriber Verification.

It should allow authorized administrators to:
- View providers requiring verification.
- Review license information, NPI, and prescribing credentials.
- Record verification source, date, and verifier.
- Approve or revoke prescribing authorization.
- View verification history.

Prescribers must not approve their own authorization, including clinic owners.

Prescribing authorization must be enforced on the server, not merely through the UI.

Do not automatically authorize providers based on their role or account ownership.
