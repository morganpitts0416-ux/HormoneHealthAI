I agree with the proposed Phase 2 direction and would like to proceed using Option A (thin routing layer). Please keep the existing channel-specific tables and avoid creating a canonical communications table at this stage.

However, I want to clarify the long-term communication architecture before building reply functionality.

Core Philosophy

I do not want staff thinking:

"Do I reply through Spruce or Portal?"

I want staff thinking:

"I'm replying to the patient."

The system should determine the delivery channel while maintaining a single communication history.

Long-Term Model

* Patient Portal Communication Timeline = complete longitudinal communication history
* Spruce = SMS delivery channel
* ClinIQ Inbox = clinic-wide communication workspace
* Patient Chart = patient-specific communication workspace

The Inbox and Patient Chart should both read from the same underlying communication model and remain synchronized automatically.

Unified Communication Timeline

The patient profile should continue evolving toward a single chronological communication timeline containing:

* Spruce SMS
* Portal messages
* Staff replies
* Spruce June messages
* System communication events
* Future communication channels

All displayed chronologically with source labels.

Reply Functionality

I would like staff to be able to reply directly from the Patient Chart as well as from the Inbox.

Default behavior:

* Use the most recently active communication channel.
* Clearly display:
  "Replying via Spruce SMS"
  or
  "Replying via ClinIQ Portal"

If only one channel exists, no channel selector is needed.

If multiple channels exist, allow staff to switch channels.

Portal as the Source of Truth

I think the patient portal should become the complete patient-visible communication history.

When a staff member replies:

1. The reply should ALWAYS be saved into the unified communication timeline.
2. The reply should ALWAYS appear in the patient’s portal communication history.
3. The reply should only be transmitted externally through Spruce if Spruce is the active or selected delivery channel.

Example:

Patient texts via Spruce:
"Can I get a refill?"

Staff replies from ClinIQ:
"Which pharmacy would you like us to use?"

Result:

* Message appears in the patient communication timeline.
* Message appears in the patient portal history.
* Message is delivered through Spruce SMS because Spruce was the active channel.

Portal Reply Example

Patient sends a Portal message.

Staff replies.

Result:

* Message appears in the communication timeline.
* Message appears in the portal.
* No Spruce SMS is sent.

Important Distinction

I do NOT want every portal reply automatically sent via Spruce.

I do NOT want every Spruce reply creating duplicate notifications.

I DO want:

* one communication history
* one timeline
* one place for staff to review patient communications
* channel-specific delivery when appropriate
* visible source labels
* complete audit history

Clinical Workflow Goal

When I am inside the patient chart reviewing:

* labs
* encounter notes
* medications
* monitoring data
* prior communications

I should be able to respond to the patient without leaving the chart.

The Inbox remains the clinic-wide communication workspace.

The Patient Chart becomes the patient-specific communication workspace.

Future Consideration

Please also think through adding a future patient-level preference field:

primaryCommunicationChannel

Examples:

* Spruce SMS
* Portal
* Email

This is not required for Phase 2 but should be considered when designing the architecture.

Safety / Scope

Keep this isolated to:

* Inbox
* Portal messaging
* Spruce messaging
* Communication timeline architecture

Do NOT modify:

* ClinIQ June
* Ask June
* SOAP generation
* transcription
* lab evaluation
* evidence overlay
* auth
* billing
* sessions
* unrelated core ClinIQ systems

Before implementation, please review the existing Portal messaging architecture and propose the cleanest technical approach for making the Patient Portal communication history the longitudinal source of truth while preserving channel-specific delivery behavior.
