Credential handling

Copy the existing bcrypt hash byte-for-byte into the identity credential record.
Preserve the original legacy hash unchanged.
Verify that the same synthetic password authenticates through both paths.
Do not use differing bcrypt hashes as proof that passwords differ.
Never attempt multiple hashes sequentially during login.
Never log passwords or hashes.
