Approved first boundary

Implement in Replit development only:

A comprehensive synthetic compatibility and revocation regression harness.
A centralized clinician/staff clinic-access resolver.
Shadow-only comparison of the centralized resolver against current authorization behavior.
Read-only inventory and reporting of existing users who would be denied.
No real enforcement and no team-management mutation endpoints yet.
Resolver requirements

The proposed resolver must:

Distinguish clinician, staff, patient-portal, integration, webhook, and system actors.
Require exact active clinic membership and active acceptance for clinicians.
Require exact active staff assignment for staff.
Never treat defaultClinicId as authorization.
Never silently fall back to an owner, clinician, or another clinic.
Recognize base-authentication-only routes such as logout and explicit clinic reselection.
Return sanitized allow, deny, or indeterminate reasons.
Make no response or session change while shadow-only.

Existing-user preservation

With all enforcement disabled:

Clinician login must remain unchanged.
Staff login must remain unchanged.
Existing sessions must remain valid.
Patient login and portal behavior must remain unchanged.
No membership, staff, provider, patient assignment, user, session, invitation, or password record may be changed.
No existing user may be required to reset a password.

Every clinician lacking a coherent active membership for their current/default clinic.

Every clinic with zero, multiple, or mismatched owners.
Every active provider lacking coherent active authority.
