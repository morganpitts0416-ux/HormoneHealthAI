MEDICATION STATE CLASSIFICATION RULES

Every medication mention must first be classified into ONE of these states:

1. ACTIVE_CURRENT
   Medication the patient is currently taking.

Requires strong evidence such as:

* “currently taking”
* “on”
* “takes”
* “using”
* “continue”
* refill discussion
* dose/frequency confirmation
* explicit medication reconciliation

2. NEWLY_PRESCRIBED
   Medication explicitly initiated during this encounter.

Requires:

* provider recommendation + plan
* dosage
* initiation language
  Examples:
* “start tirzepatide 2.5 mg weekly”
* “will initiate”
* “prescribed”

3. HISTORICAL
   Previously used medication, not current.

Examples:

* “previously took”
* “used in the past”
* “was on”
* “had success with”

4. DISCUSSED_ONLY
   Medication discussed for comparison, education, counseling, side effects, differential discussion, or hypothetical future consideration.

Examples:

* “like phentermine”
* “some patients use…”
* “if mood worsens we could consider…”
* “discussed stimulant versus GLP-1 medications”

DISCUSSED_ONLY medications must NEVER populate:

* current meds
* active medication list
* medication reconciliation
* discharge meds
* treatment plan
  unless later explicitly prescribed.

5. UNCERTAIN
   Insufficient evidence to determine medication state.

UNCERTAIN medications must NOT be added to the active medication list automatically.

Additional safety rules:

1. Medication mentions inside educational counseling or comparisons are NOT active medications.

2. Medication names appearing without explicit patient usage confirmation should default to DISCUSSED_ONLY, not ACTIVE_CURRENT.

3. Do not infer medication usage from conversational context alone.

4. The medication reconciliation section should be conservative and evidence-based.

5. When uncertain, omit from active medications rather than hallucinate.

6. Add a final medication hallucination check before note finalization:

   * compare active meds against explicit transcript evidence
   * remove unsupported medications
   * flag inferred-only medications internally
