Before we investigate the remaining Patient Communication selection behavior, I want to add one small but important provider-control layer to the resolved Supplement Protocol.

Currently the Brain assigns each supplement a priority (high/medium/low), but the provider should be able to override that priority while curating the patient's Supplement Protocol.

Please analyze the smallest architecture change needed to allow provider priority overrides. Do not implement yet.

Desired behavior:

The Brain continues assigning the initial/default priority.
In the provider Supplement Protocol UI, the provider can change any recommendation between High, Medium, and Low priority.
The provider-selected priority becomes part of the persisted protocol decision for that patient/lab evaluation.
The shared resolved patient-visible Supplement Protocol should expose the provider-selected priority when one exists; otherwise use the Brain priority.
This final resolved priority should be the priority received by the Patient Communication writer and any other consumer of the resolved protocol.
Hiding/unhiding a supplement remains independent from changing its priority.
Clinician-added supplements should also support High/Medium/Low priority.
Do not change the underlying Brain supplement-selection/scoring algorithms.

Priority semantics for Patient Communication:

High: Core component of the provider's treatment plan. When there is a clinically relevant treatment domain in the Health Assessment, the writer should integrate the product naturally into that discussion. High priority should strongly favor inclusion, not create a separate supplement list.
Medium: Recommended and clinically relevant. Include when it materially helps explain the treatment plan, but narrative inclusion is not required.
Low: Supportive/optional protocol recommendation. Usually leave it to the full Supplement Protocol unless it is particularly important to understanding the plan.

High priority does not mean mechanically listing every high-priority supplement. The writer should integrate it into the finding/pattern it addresses:

finding/pattern → meaning → treatment plan → relevant high-priority supplement → monitoring/follow-up

The existing fidelity rules remain:

exact resolved product and dose when mentioned;
no generic substitutions;
no independently changed doses;
no supplement absent from the resolved protocol;
no contradiction of the provider-curated protocol.

Also preserve the current provider-to-patient narrative goal: this is a cohesive explanation of the treatment plan, not another copy of the Supplement Protocol.

Before implementing, show me:

where priority is currently generated and stored;
whether provider supplement overrides already have a suitable place to persist a priority override;
the smallest schema/data/UI/resolver changes needed;
how the final provider-selected priority would reach the Patient Communication writer;
whether this can be added without changing Brain supplement-selection logic.
