---
name: Access rollout compatibility gate
description: Durable safety constraints for subscription lifecycle, recovery, and multi-clinic patient authorization work.
---

The legacy access path remains authoritative while proposed authorization is measured in shadow mode. Migration or backfill completion must never activate enforcement. Ambiguous owner, billing, membership, portal identity, or patient-clinic records stay on the legacy path and enter review rather than being guessed, merged, linked, reassigned, or disabled. Narrow approved exceptions are tenant-scoped chart/workflow reads and Group 1 patient-linked lab interpretation, lab-summary regeneration, and AI chat. Public raw-value lab interpretation remains intentional but must use a recursive strict allowlist, reject identifying/free-form/unknown/prototype keys, and perform no patient-data reads or writes.

**Why:** Incorrect denials could interrupt care or lock patients out of their own records, while incorrect grants could expose cross-clinic PHI. Identity similarity and billing state are evidence, not authorization on their own.

**How to apply:** Keep every other enforcement and production-UX flag disabled until the corresponding report is explicitly approved. For approved patient-linked routes, revalidate current active membership/staff assignment rather than trusting stale session clinic IDs, then scope patient, chart, lab, vitals, supplement, and write queries in SQL. A logged-in actor without current authority may use only the same public raw-value path as an anonymous caller. Return identical non-disclosing not-found responses for foreign and nonexistent resources. Patient-clinic relationships require explicit verification; never create them from matching email, demographics, or legacy patient IDs alone.
